01 / The room

A small dining room, kept by the people who cook in it.

2likeus sits in a Victorian terrace on Castle Road — the residential ribbon that runs north from the Embankment, away from the High Street noise. The room is modest. The hosts are not.

Bookings are taken by message — the easiest route is our Facebook page, where the kitchen also posts when the menu changes.

03 / Getting here

Castle Road, just past the bend.

Coming from the river, walk up The Embankment, turn left onto Castle Road and keep going. We’re on the left — a quiet shopfront, easy to miss if you’re not looking. Parking is on-street; the bus stops on St Cuthbert’s Street are five minutes away.
